## Why

  with native correction paths and metered cost sources.

  customer's warehouse.

  (`go/assay/settlement/` absent). Phase 14 has no subject.

  support without runtime machinery).

  system that does own its outcomes.
- Everything else (routing thesis, DAG/materialization runtime, replanning,
  planners, outcome ledgers) stays rejected. The honest product shape, if
  any exists, is small, technical, and inside systems that already own their
  outcomes — not a new system of record.
